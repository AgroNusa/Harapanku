# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Project: Harapanku — Pediatric Growth & Therapy Clinic Digital Platform
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved / In Execution  
**Target Region:** Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, Indonesia  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**Harapanku** is a modern, premium, and trustworthy child development clinic (*Klinik Tumbuh Kembang Anak*) tailored for busy, professional parents. The clinic offers multidisciplinary therapy services including ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis), Speech Therapy (Wicara), Occupational Therapy (Okupasi), and Comprehensive Pediatric Psychological Assessments.

### Vision Statement
Democratize transparent, timely, and empathetic pediatric intervention without months-long waiting lists, empowering parents through real-time developmental data tracking and a warm, clinical-grade healing environment.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
### Primary Persona: "Bunda Rina" (The Concerned Professional Parent)
* **Demographics:** Working mother or father, age 28–42, resident of Balikpapan or surrounding areas.
* **Pain Points:**
  * Anxiety over child milestone delays (speech delay, sensory overload, tantrums).
  * Frustrated by typical hospital waiting lists ranging from 2 to 6 months.
  * Opaque therapy processes with no clear visibility into what happens inside the therapy room.
  * Overwhelmed by lengthy clinical registration forms while juggling work.
* **Goals:**
  * Secure prompt, high-quality diagnostic assessment.
  * Observe and measure tangible progress week-over-week.
  * Feel emotionally supported by certified, warm practitioners.

---

## 3. Product Goals & Core Metrics (KPIs)
1. **Conversion Rate (Landing Page to Assessment Lead):** Target ≥ 8.5% through frictionless UX.
2. **Time to First Assessment:** Average booking lead time < 3 business days (Zero Waiting List promise).
3. **Parent Engagement:** ≥ 75% weekly active rate on the Parent Dashboard.
4. **Trust & Retention:** Net Promoter Score (NPS) ≥ +65 among enrolled families.

---

## 4. Brand Identity & Design System Standards

### 4.1 Psychology of Color Palette
* **Primary — Soft Medical Blue (`#4A90E2` / `#357ABD`):** Establishes clinical credibility, medical expertise, and trust without the cold, intimidating feel of standard hospitals.
* **Secondary — Sage Green (`#8FBC8F`):** Symbolizes holistic growth, balance, and reassurance, lowering parent anxiety.
* **Accent — Warm Golden Orange (`#F5A623`):** Exclusively reserved for primary conversion triggers (Call to Action buttons) for maximum visual contrast and actionability.
* **Neutral Surface (`#F9F9FF` / `#FFFFFF`):** Warm pearl off-white background with soft elevation shadows, preventing cognitive clutter.

### 4.2 Typography System
* **Headings:** `Poppins` / `Nunito` (rounded sans-serif, warm, child-friendly, friendly authority).
* **Body & Data:** `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (ultra-readable, clean, crisp metrics rendering).

### 4.3 Visual Asset Guidelines
* Authentic, unposed documentary-style photography of Asian children and certified therapists interacting with wooden Montessori sensory toys.
* Soft organic blobs, curved edges (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`), avoiding sharp or rigid hospital grids.

## 5. Information Architecture & Page Anatomy

### 5.1 Global Navigation (Navbar)
* **Logo:** Harapanku emblem with parent-and-child supportive motif.
* **Quick Links:** Layanan Terapi, Alur Konsultasi, Fasilitas, Paket Asesmen, Parent Dashboard, FAQ.
* **Global Action:** CTA Button *"Daftar Asesmen Awal"*.

### 5.2 Hero Section (Value Proposition & Trust Anchor)
* **Headline:** *"Ketahui Kebutuhan Tumbuh Kembang Anak Anda Hari Ini."*
* **Value Props:** Zero waitlists, integrated multidisciplinary therapy, transparent digital monitoring.
* **Trust Badges:** Verified STR Kemenkes therapists, accredited facility in Balikpapan, 4.9/5 rating from 350+ parents.
* **Primary Actions:** Primary Button *"Jadwalkan Asesmen Awal"* + Secondary Direct WhatsApp Link.

### 5.3 Clinical Advantages ("Mengapa Ratusan Orang Tua Mempercayakan Kami?")
1. **Terapi Terintegrasi Komprehensif:** ABA, Wicara, and Okupasi aligned under one unified developmental roadmap.
2. **Parent Dashboard Terbuka:** Real-time visibility into therapist notes, session photos, and metric progression.
3. **Terapis Tersertifikasi & Hangat:** University-trained licensed clinicians using play-based interventions.

### 5.4 Specialized Therapy Programs
Detailed service cards featuring session duration, targets, and clinical scopes:
* **Asesmen Awal Komprehensif (90 min):** Motoric, speech, cognitive milestone diagnosis & parent counseling.
* **Terapi Perilaku / ABA (60 min):** Positive reinforcement, instructional focus, tantrum regulation.
* **Terapi Wicara / Speech Therapy (45 min):** Articulation clarity, expressive vocabulary, oral-motor support.
* **Terapi Okupasi / Occupational Therapy (60 min):** Sensory integration, balance, vestibular & fine-motor coordination.

### 5.5 How It Works (Structured 3-Step Roadmap)
1. **Langkah 1 — Asesmen Psikologi & Observasi:** Holistic evaluation in a cozy, non-threatening play environment.
2. **Langkah 2 — Penyusunan Roadmap Personal:** Personalized clinical roadmap formulated collaboratively with parents.
3. **Langkah 3 — Sesi Terapi & Evaluasi Berkala:** 1-on-1 therapeutic sessions with milestone reviews every 12 sessions.

### 5.6 Transparency Technology (Parent Dashboard Preview)
* Interactive preview showing:
  * Milestone Radar & progress metrics (e.g., Expressive Speech 82%, Sensory Integration 75%).
  * 8-week independence growth trend curve.
  * Daily Therapy Log snippet authored by the clinician at the end of each session.

### 5.7 Clinic Facilities Gallery ("Fasilitas Ramah Anak & Nyaman Seperti di Rumah")
* High-res imagery and descriptions:
  * **Ruang Sensori Integrasi (SI):** Suspended sensory swings, impact-absorbing safety floor mats, tactile walls.
  * **Ruang Terapi Wicara:** Ergonomic child-sized wooden desks, articulation mirrors, distraction-free sound insulation.
  * **Ruang Observasi & Konsultasi Ortu:** Cozy Montessori observation room with family sofas for private clinical debriefs.

### 5.8 Assessment Pricing Packages (Transparent 2-Tier Pricing)
* **Tier 1 — Screening Tumbuh Kembang Dasar (IDR 350,000):**
  * 45-minute developmental screening (Denver II / M-CHAT guidelines).
  * Written PDF evaluation report and introductory therapy orientation.
* **Tier 2 — Asesmen Komprehensif Multidisiplin (IDR 750,000) [Recommended]:**
  * 90-minute in-depth multi-aspect evaluation (Sensory, Speech, Adaptive behavior).
  * Comprehensive clinical diagnostic report & personalized milestone roadmap.
  * 1-on-1 dedicated 30-minute debrief with Licensed Child Psychologist.
  * Bonus 1-month free access to Parent Dashboard.

### 5.9 FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions Accordion)
* Proactively addresses key hesitation barriers:
  * Immediate availability without waiting lists (scheduling within 1–3 business days).
  * Legal credentials and national certifications of pediatric therapists.
  * Eligible age range (1–12 years old).
  * Open door observation policy for parents.

### 5.10 Conversion & Frictionless UX Strategy
* **Minimalist Intake Modal:** Eliminates lengthy medical intake forms. Collects only:
  1. Parent Name (Ayah/Bunda)
  2. Child Age
  3. Primary Concern (Dropdown: Speech Delay, Sensory/Motoric, ABA/Behavioral, General Observation)
  4. WhatsApp Number
* Instant handover to Human Care Coordinator for warm WhatsApp consultation.

## 6. Technical Stack & Infrastructure
* **Frontend:** Tailwind CSS, responsive mobile-first responsive layout, accessible semantic HTML5.
* **Interactive Elements:** Accessible modals, collapsible accordions, SVG responsive data charts.
* **Integration Channels:** WhatsApp Business API click-to-chat with pre-filled referral metadata.
* **Hosting & Security:** High-speed edge CDN with HTTPS compliance for sensitive developmental inquiry data.
